## Hello, I'm Sylvester.

I'm a cybersecurity professional with more than seven years of experience across security operations, offensive testing, monitoring, and incident response. My interests are centered on **penetration testing, web application security, and bug bounty research**—with an emphasis on turning technical findings into clear, actionable recommendations.

I approach security work methodically: understand the environment, identify meaningful weaknesses, validate the risk, and communicate the results in a way that teams can use.

### Professional experience

**OffSec — Student Mentor (2018–2024)**

At OffSec, I supported a large community of cybersecurity learners and worked closely with technical teams:

- Mentored **1,000+ students** on cybersecurity concepts through OffSec's community platform.
- Supported and maintained lab and examination environments used by **1,000+ users**.
- Reviewed technical exam reports across OSCP, OSWP, OSWA, and OSWE programs.
- Helped test examination environments, improve documentation, and support operational processes.

Earlier roles in network engineering, data centers, and IT support helped build my foundation in infrastructure, systems, and troubleshooting. That background informs how I assess security issues today: understanding how technology works is essential to explaining how it can fail.
